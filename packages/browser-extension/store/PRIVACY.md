# Walnut browser extension: privacy policy

The Walnut extension connects your browser to the Walnut server you run yourself (on your own
computer, or a machine you set up). It talks to that server and to no one else.

**What it handles.** Page text you ask about in the side panel; the logins and one-time codes it
fills from your Walnut vault; the address, text and screenshots of tabs your Walnut agents use when
you turn agents on; the steps those agents take there.

**Where it goes.** Only to your own Walnut server, over the connection you set up with Connect. The
developer of Walnut receives nothing: there are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no
third-party services in the extension.

**What it keeps.** In the browser, only what it needs to work: which Walnut it is connected to, its
settings, and which tabs agents hold. Your vault stays encrypted on your Walnut server and is
unlocked with your master password.

**Your control.** Agents can use your browser only after you turn it on in the extension's
Settings; every agent tab has Stop and Take over. Disconnect in the extension, or Remove in Walnut's
Settings, Browser extension, ends its access at once. Uninstalling the extension deletes what it
kept in the browser.

**Questions.** Open an issue at https://github.com/EvanZhang008/open-walnut/issues.
